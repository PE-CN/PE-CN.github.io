# [Problem 482](https://projecteuler.net/problem=482)
***
**The incenter of a triangle**

ABC is an integer sided triangle with incenter I and perimeter p.
The segments IA, IB and IC have integral length as well. 

Let L = p + |IA| + |IB| + |IC|. 

Let S(P) = ∑L for all such triangles where p ≤ P. For example, S(10<sup>3</sup>) = 3619.

Find S(10<sup>7</sup>).
***
**三角形的内心**

三角形ABC的三边长都是整数，其内心记为I，周长记为p.
连接内心和顶点的线段IA，IB和IC的长度恰好也都是整数。 

记L = p + |IA| + |IB| + |IC|。 

对于所有满足p ≤ P的三角形，记S(P) = ∑L。例如，S(10<sup>3</sup>) = 3619。

求S(10<sup>7</sup>)。
