**Sliding game**

In a sliding game a counter may slide horizontally or vertically into an empty space. The objective of the game is to move the red counter from the top left corner of a grid to the bottom right corner; the space always starts in the bottom right corner. For example, the following sequence of pictures show how the game can be completed in five moves on a 2 by 2 grid.

<center><img src="/resources/images/0313_sliding_game_1.gif"></center>

Let S(m,n) represent the minimum number of moves to complete the game on an m by n grid. For example, it can be verified that S(5,4) = 25.

<center><img src="/resources/images/0313_sliding_game_2.gif"></center>

There are exactly 5482 grids for which S(m,n) = p<sup>2</sup>, where p &lt; 100 is prime.

How many grids does S(m,n) = p<sup>2</sup>, where p &lt; 10<sup>6</sup> is prime?

在滑动游戏中，筹码可以水平或竖直地划入相邻的空位。游戏的目标是将网格左上角的红色筹码移动到右下角；开始时空格总是在右下角。下面的一系列图片演示了如何用五步完成2乘2网格的游戏。

<center><img src="/resources/images/0313_sliding_game_1.gif"></center>

记S(m,n)为完成m乘n网格的游戏所需的最少步数。例如，可以验证S(5,4) = 25。

<center><img src="/resources/images/0313_sliding_game_2.gif"></center>

一共有5482种网格使得S(m,n) = p<sup>2</sup>，其中p &lt; 100是素数。

有多少种网格使得S(m,n) = p<sup>2</sup>，其中p &lt; 10<sup>6</sup>是素数？
