## **Straight Eight**

In Poker a **straight** is exactly five cards of sequential rank NOT all of the same suit.
In this problem an ace can rank either high as in A-K-Q-J-10 or low as in 5-4-3-2-A, but cannot simultaneously rank both high and low, so Q-K-A-2-3 is not allowed.

There are $10200$ ways of choosing a straight from a normal $52$ card deck.<br>
There are $31832952$ ways of choosing two disjoint straights from a single $52$ card deck.

Find the number of ways of choosing eight disjoint straights from a single $52$ card deck.<br>
In this the order of choosing the straights does not matter.

## **八手顺子**

在扑克牌中，**顺子**指五张点数连续且花色不完全相同的牌。在本题中，A既可以作为最大牌出现在A-K-Q-J-10中，也可以作为最小牌出现在5-4-3-2-A中，但不能同时充当最大牌和最小牌，因此Q-K-A-2-3是不合法的。

从一副标准的$52$张扑克牌中选出一手顺子，共有$10200$种方式。<br>
从一副标准的$52$张扑克牌中选出两手顺子（不能用相同的牌），共有$31832952$种方式。

求从一副标准的$52$张扑克牌中选出八手顺子（不能用相同的牌）的方式数目。<br>
注意本题中选取各手顺子时不计先后顺序。
