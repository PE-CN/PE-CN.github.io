**Squares under a hyperbola**

Consider the region constrained by 1 ≤ x and 0 ≤ y ≤ <sup>1</sup>/<sub>x</sub>.

Let S<sub>1</sub> be the largest square that can fit under the curve.
Let S<sub>2</sub> be the largest square that fits in the remaining area, and so on. 
Let the <i>index</i> of S<sub>n</sub> be the pair (left, below) indicating the number of squares to the left of S<sub>n</sub> and the number of squares below S<sub>n</sub>.

<center><img src="/resources/images/0247_hypersquares.gif" alt=""></center>

The diagram shows some such squares labelled by number. 
S<sub>2</sub> has one square to its left and none below, so the index of S<sub>2</sub> is (1,0).
It can be seen that the index of S<sub>32</sub> is (1,1) as is the index of S<sub>50</sub>. 
50 is the largest n for which the index of S<sub>n</sub> is (1,1).

What is the largest n for which the index of S<sub>n</sub> is (3,3)?

**双曲线下的正方形**

考虑由1 ≤ x和0 ≤ y ≤ <sup>1</sup>/<sub>x</sub>所表示的区域。

记S<sub>1</sub>是能够放进曲线下区域的最大正方形。
记S<sub>2</sub>是能够放进剩余区域的最大正方形，并依此类推。
S<sub>n</sub>的<i>索引</i>是一个数对(left, below)，分别表示S<sub>n</sub>左侧的正方形数目和S<sub>n</sub>下放的正方形数目。

<center><img src="/resources/images/0247_hypersquares.gif" alt=""></center>

上图展示了一些这样的正方形，并标上了相应的数字顺序。
S<sub>2</sub>的左侧有一个正方形，下方没有正方形，因此S<sub>2</sub>的索引为(1,0)。
可以看出S<sub>32</sub>的索引和S<sub>50</sub>的索引同为(1,1)。 
使得S<sub>n</sub>索引为(1,1)的数n最大是50。

使得S<sub>n</sub>索引为(3,3)的数n最大是多少？
