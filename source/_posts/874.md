## **Maximal Prime Score**

Let $p(t)$ denote the $(t+1)$th prime number. So that $p(0) = 2$, $p(1) = 3$, etc.<br />
We define the <i>prime score</i> of a list of nonnegative integers $[a_1, \dots, a_n]$ as the sum $\sum_{i = 1}^n p(a_i)$.<br />
Let $M(k, n)$ be the maximal prime score among all lists $[a_1, \dots, a_n]$ such that:

- $0 \leq a_i &lt; k$ for each $i$;
- the sum $\sum_{i = 1}^n a_i$ is a multiple of $k$.

For example, $M(2, 5) = 14$ as $[0, 1, 1, 1, 1]$ attains a maximal prime score of $14$.

Find $M(7000, p(7000))$.

## **最大质数得分**

记$p(t)$为第$(t+1)$个质数，因此$p(0) = 2$，$p(1) = 3$，依此类推。<br />
定义非负整数列$[a_1, \dots, a_n]$的<i class=zh>质数得分</i>为$\sum_{i = 1}^n p(a_i)$。<br />
考虑所有满足如下条件的非负整数列$[a_1, \dots, a_n]$，记$M(k,n)$为其质数得分的最大值：

- 对于所有$i$，$0 \leq a_i < k$；
- 数列和$\sum_{i = 1}^n a_i$是$k$的倍数。

例如，$M(2, 5) = 14$，最大质数得分$14$在数列$[0, 1, 1, 1, 1]$时取得。

求$M(7000, p(7000))$。
